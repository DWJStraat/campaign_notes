A restfull night slowly transitions into a sort of vision which draws you to your homeland- Tymanther. In the capital of Tymanther, I would find myself, seeing the upper echelons of the city, where I used to live. Before the Watchers of Chronepsis. But I would be in the lower parts of the city, which I am unfamiliar with. I behold two individuals, conversing. Scheming? A gold dragonborn, with 6 bone ivory studs on the left side of their face, though lacking any other markings. A coppery dragonborn, with fang-shaped studs in their frills, and under their tunic is a symbol shaped like a dark-red rose with five pronounced thorns. Around the rose is a red dragon. I recognise this as the symbol of the Knights of the Five-Thorned Rose, an organisation tasked with the destruction of Bahamut and his children. It is tied to Clan Vkriss. They are devoted to Tiamat.


The coppery dragonborn speaks: "There was a dragon. My family is interested in their wares. They are called [[Rotwind]], I believe. They are offering a weapon of sorts- the Living Dragon Plague, to our clan specifically. Apparently her child controls a great deal of poisons in Baldur's Gate. Though, frankly, our clan's experiencing a bit of a split between utilizing it to kill all tyrant-wyrms, or just wiping out the metallic dragons"

The vision fades, before returning to a conversation between Diartesh Daardendrien, my mentor and teacher. They are talking to an unseen figure. "We must let Dorn know that there is a Shadow Dragon active near Baldur's Gate. We have heard of them specifically serving Felazure, by the name of [[Vazuth]]. I will let Dorn know of this development. See what we can achieve. Prepare for the worst. You should keep you talks with the Vkriss going for as long as can be."

The vision fades, and I drift to sleep.

I wake up, and I find a piece of paper in my lap, written in Draconic.

Below is the translation

---
Kepeshmolik Dorn, 

We give to you dreams gathered through a Vkriss that betrays their family, yet for our gain. clan Daardendrien spies in the house of Vkriss. Be watchful for the Vkriss just out of sight. 

As for you, your time is close at hand. Your rival has made her move in the Gate of Baldur, a Dragon that is heresy to the laws your father has set in place, a heretic to Chronepsis. Now you must make yours, this Wyrm lies between undead and life, and so find the way to right this wrongness, and a weapon of our temple shall find its place at your side. 

Signed, Diartesh Daardendrien, Cembetel Kepeshmolik
