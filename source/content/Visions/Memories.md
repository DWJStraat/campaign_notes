I find my mind drifting back to the House of Null- of Chronepsis. The lessons of the temple, of my mentor, on how to put a dragon to rest, pass through my mind- a reflection of today's events. Similarly, I think back to the visions of the shadow dragons in Baldurs Gate, the worship of Felazure. It goes back to the ritual of the Cult of the Dragon, and the individual with single red eye. 

I drift further into memories. There is one more, though it lacks familiarity. It is a memory of a larger body, a decrepit body. A body that has seen scarring beyond anything. It is a body of a strange color. It is not a chromatic or metallic dragon, but a dark-purple gem dragon. Though the specific name escapes me. 

I feel the body. It feels old, and weak- which is not a normal thing for dragons. It seems the body is dying, or dead. I am in an underground complex. It is a great cavern. I see glints of gold and gems and treasures and corpses of dragons wrapped in linen and embalmed. A few individuals cluster around me. 

One Dragonborn stands above them, others are of the softer races, mostly humans. They walk around my fallen form. So tiny. I would estimate I am between Huge and Gargantuan size. They walk past enchantments crackling with blue energies; wards of some kind. I notice, I am being embalmed. The process is long, but passes by in the mists of dreams. Eventually I am lowered into a crypt. One of these individuals has a mace, and I feel a tinge of familiarity. A tinge of divine connection for a split second, as my soul departs with the words spoken, to be raised to Chronepsis, Falazure, to Null. To join him in the afterlife reserved for me. But a part of me is drawn to this mace. 

I am drawn to another memory. I see my mentor, congratulating me on memorizing the rites necessary to put a dragon to rest. "It is good that you practice this well. I hope you never have to see this death, but I am sure you will perform quite well."

I wake up in the middle of the night. There is weight on my chest. It is getting familiar. A heavy mace rests on my chest.
