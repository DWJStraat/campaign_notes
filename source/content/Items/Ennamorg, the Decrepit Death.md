*Requires Attunement*

This +2 Mace can be used as a spellcasting focus. While attuned, it grants the following benefits:
- You always have the *Augury* and *Ceremony* spell prepared, and can cast *Augury* once per long rest without requiring material components.
- If you lay a True Dragon to rest using the *Funeral Rite* feature from the *Ceremony* spell, it gains a charge. The mace currently has 3 charges

**Sentient**

The mace contains the soul of Ennamorg, granting it sentience. Ennamorg speaks Draconic, but can only communicate with it's wielder through a 60 ft range of empathy, but only to creatures who speak Draconic. Ennamorg has 10 ft blindsight. When the wielder casts the *Ceremony* spell, they can choose to forego any benefits from the spell to instead allow Ennamorg to speak. Ennamorg has extensive knowledge on the topics of Dragons, Undeath, and Null.

 _Approval._ Ennamorg's trust and your favour with Null can unlock further abilities of the mace. Once appropriate, you gain favour, you can spend this on abilities that shall be revealed once the the favour necessary to unlock them becomes available.

**The following abilities have been unlocked:**

1 Favour - While attuned, you gain resistance to fire damage. 

1 Favour - While attuned, you always have the _Animate Undead_ spell prepared, and you can cast it once without using a spell slot once per long rest.
