Mission assignment, high-priority target: [[Scarlet Vandran]] 

[[Scarlet Vandran]] shall be your next target. She used to be a Priest of [[Cyric]] in the service to [[Manshoon]], almost directly. Her goals and the [[Zhentarim]]'s goals conflicted, and so it was devised that she should fall in service to [[Cyric]]. At last she has not, and she's made an escape. You are allowed to use any means necessary, and should pick up the trail as soon as can be. Where she is we do not know, but you will no doubt figure this out.
