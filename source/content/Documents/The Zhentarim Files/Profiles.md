# **Profile: [[Banites]]** 
Transcribed by [[Wendell Roseberg]], translated from Deep Speech 

Regarding the danger posed by the "[[Banites]]" 

My Lord [[Manshoon]], 

The Church of [[Bane]] has been an interesting thorn in the [[Zhentarim]]'s side, ever since the more religious aspects of our organisation have dedicated themselves to the Church of [[Cyric]], something you no doubt find better, easier to control, than [[Bane]] itself. [[Fzul Chembryl|Fzoul]] was always a problem after all. While recently their numbers have dwindled, they are far from exorcised from the city. Rumours speak of important priests visiting, and picking up their fued with the [[Zhentarim]] once more. 

By the decree of a Dread Lord of Baldur's Gate, [[Thautoque]]
# **Profile: [[Blightbreath]]**
 
 Transcribed by [[Wendell Roseberg]], translated from Deep Speech 
 
 Regarding "[[Blightbreath]]" 
 
 My Lord [[Manshoon]], 
 
 An individual who has shown quite some promise as a market force, this Dragon is one well-versed in subterfuge, yet might be in over his head. They've been a known rival to the [[Zhentarim]] and [[The Guild|Guild]] poison market, though that is where they remain. It's remarkably interesting how they seem to be of an ambitious sort, yet they don't overlap into other territories. Perhaps they truly are satisfied with the business and the riches they gain from it. Or they are content to leech off of the quality of their most talented poisoner. 
 
 By the decree of a Dread Lord of Baldur's Gate, [[Thautoque]]

# **Profile: [[Ninefingers|Nine-Fingers]] & [[The Guild|Guild]]** 

Transcribed by [[Wendell Roseberg]], translated from Deep Speech 

Regarding "[[Ninefingers|Nine-Fingers Keene]]" and the "[[The Guild|Guild]]" 

My Lord [[Manshoon]], 

[[Ninefingers|Nine-Fingers Keene]] is a criminal which can be manipulated in emotional ways if need be. While a career criminal, and quite one at that, she does carry some sense of sentimentality, and is more alien to the ruthlessness that the [[Zhentarim]] possess. This is both a weakness and a strength, it is weak in that it makes one vulnerable, yet the powers at play are more willing to let her slip through her fingers as long as she ascribes to a degree of morals. It is just a matter of how far those morals stretch, when does something become too dangerous to touch. She is undoubtedly a roadblock to the [[Zhentarim]], and open war against her is an inevitability. We merely need to go about pursuing this war as a matter of last resorts, open war should not be risked unless there are no other paths open to us. 

There is also the matter of her resourcefulness. [[The Guild]] itself are integrated in a great many things, and would be the primary rival of the [[Zhentarim]], besides the matter of the institutions of Baldur's Gate trying to maintain autonomy. 

By the decree of a Dread Lord of Baldur's Gate, [[Thautoque]]
