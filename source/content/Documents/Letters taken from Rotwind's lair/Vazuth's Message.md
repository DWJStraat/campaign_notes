My _noachi Marux_, 

I guarantee to you that I shall safeguard your treasures with the most care, the Cloakwood is safe under my protection, I will be ever vigilant as Klauth. I believe this is necessary for the time being, unfortunately, at least until Baldur's Gate has been secured, and Kaldir has made the final arrangements for the cargo's transport. Then I may be called upon, my love, for all that you require. 

If you can afford to, please do savour the _darathric_ of the Kepeshmolik _vethindas_, otherwise I would much like to savour him. Falazure would much like me to prove my worth against his son. If only one could eliminate the part of him that Chronepsis has sired. 

Your _Ingeir_
