We offer our condolences to the merry band who have met [[Gimmun Bearhammer|Gimmun]] and shared in hunts with him, we similarly lament the loss of him in our own court, as his fickle nature might have lifted our spirits. We have heard his belongings have found a suitable place, and shall arrange his funeral, in accordance with his lord's wishes. 


Though this message does not reach you purely to offer to condolences. We have worse tidings, as someone who was amenable to the [[Summer Court]] has not sent word in a long while, and after inspection, we have realised that they have suffered an attack. Two times their grove suffered an incursion, once to kill an individual and presumably capture another, and once to capture the individual known to you as [[Bauble-eyed Bessie|'Bobble-Eyed' Bessie]]. We have similarly lost any trace of the enchantments put upon her. 


Finally, the [[Summer Court|Court]] has decided on the dissolution of the Huntington Court following these events. They have not made any progress on any of the tasks they set out to do, and so their failure has been recorded and members have been distributed among the [[Summer Court]] for better tasks.  


Instead, considering what we have heard of the exploits of [[Eleanor|Elanor Irons]], along with the [[My Favorite Band of Misfits|individuals who have worked with her]], we have decided to give her our blessing. We've relocated some funds to you, and we've included wrapped within this parchment a whistle which shall bear the blessing of Cerunnos. Do not use this lightly, or so pay the price. 


Written at the behest of Emmantiensien, who oversees the dissolution of the Huntington Court, and in name of the Horned Lord, who has a particular interest in the matter.
