[[Kepeshkmolik Dorn]], 

Yth majak ekess wux wurunwaic dryica erekess vi Vkriss batobot chianic asta svihelen, sjerit for hesi trenul. clvin Daardendrien ikni persvek wer quellar di Vkriss. Qe thesekel for wer Vkriss filki ekik di mlaen. 

Lae for wux, dout tairais ui trelk sva cha'sid. dout silah tepohaic xurwka jacioniv zhen persvek wer fesi di baldur, vi draushum batobot ui todith ekess wer laws dout opsola tepohaic seilor persvek goawy, vi todithar ekess chronepsis. jaka wux zklaen xurwk douta, nomeno ithael lapietic rionib nurnelith kagh tobor, kagh zyak ehtah wer idol ekess harkt nomeno xuutra, kagh vi esaj di hesi heeka nishka ehtah coita goawy sva dout symba. 

quilinsa, daardendrien diartesh , kepeshmolik cembetel 

Translation:

Kepeshmolik Dorn, 
We give to you dreams gathered through a Vkriss that betrays their family, yet for our gain. clan Daardendrien spies in the house of Vkriss. Be watchful for the Vkriss just out of sight. 

As for you, your time is close at hand. Your rival has made her move in the Gate of Baldur, a Dragon that is heresy to the laws your father has set in place, a heretic to Chronepsis. Now you must make yours, this Wyrm lies between undead and life, and so find the way to right this wrongness, and a weapon of our temple shall find its place at your side. 

Signed, Daardendrien Diartesh, Kepeshmolik Cembetel
