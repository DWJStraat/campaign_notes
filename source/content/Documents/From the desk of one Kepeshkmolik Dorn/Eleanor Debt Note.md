Stafford lends Eleanor 400 GP

--------
I, Kepeshkmolik Dorn, Son and Chosen of Null, herald of the Eyes of Chronepsis, herea, on the 11th day of the month of Flamerule of the year 1493, the Year of the Purple Dragons, hereafter "today", do bear witness to the following loan, extended by Stafford, hereafter "the debt holder", and Eleanor, hereafter "the debtor".

Today, the debt holder extended a loan of four hundred (400) gold pieces to the debtor for the express purpose of buying an object of mystical nature named the Elven Harness. This was made under the explicit and implicit understanding that the debtor will repay the debt holder in a reasonable time, herein defined as a period of three weeks.
