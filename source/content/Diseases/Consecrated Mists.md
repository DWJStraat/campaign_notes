A sentient magical plague. 

[[Harrow]] was paid to research it by [[Garro Skann]]

It spreads like a mist, and drains the life of infected creatures, inducing rapid necrosis

Liquifies insides, which they exhale

Light/radiance repels it

> [!NOTE] Mechanical knowledge
> 
> Likely causes necromantic damage
> 
> 1d4 necrotic
> 
> 13 > CON DC > 7, success halves
> 
> Does not function like a disease, functions more akin to a gas attack, like Cloudkill
